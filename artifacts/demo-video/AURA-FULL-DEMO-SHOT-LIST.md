## Editing rules

- Keep each pointer movement slow and intentional.
- Leave 2–5 seconds after every meaningful state change.
- Keep 15–25 seconds on opening and closing frames for narration.
- Cut dead network/model waits, but preserve enough progression to prove the
  deterministic, local, and cloud stages are distinct.
- Do not add provider-error footage unless explaining fallback is intentional.
- Do not show API keys, `.env`, Terminal, developer tools, or raw logs.
- Do not add baked-in subtitles or narration.
- Preserve the original website’s identity and AURA’s native UI text.
- Export a high-quality edit master plus a smaller sharing copy.
